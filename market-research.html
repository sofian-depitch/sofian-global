The deliverable

What's inside each report.

Each study is written for one company, its product and its target market. It uses cited market data and direct contact with local companies. You receive it as a detailed slide deck.

Market overview and trends

Market size, growth and the trends that matter for the product, with named sources.

Target market segments

The size of each segment, where the product fits best, and who is likely to buy first.

Market access and regulation

Entry routes, certifications, duties, and the regulatory path, mapped before money is spent.

Competitive landscape

Local and global competitors compared on price, positioning, revenue and headquarters, and the gap the company can use.

Potential partners and distributors

Local partners contacted before delivery, with the contact person and their level of interest.

Process

What happens in the 12 weeks.

The scope is fixed, and I plan the schedule around your program calendar and reporting deadlines.

01 01

Kick-off meeting

Week 1

An interview with each startup about its product and assets, international strategy, target market, competitors, barriers to entry and regulation. Together we set the questions the study must answer.

02 02

Research and report

Weeks 2 to 8

I research the target market and contact potential partners. You receive the report as an English slide deck.

03 03

Review meeting

Week 9

We go through the findings with the startup and your program team, and agree on the next steps for market entry.

04 04

Business matching

Weeks 10 to 12

I set up 2 to 3 meetings with potential partners from the report, and I attend them with the startup.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which target markets can you cover?
I have delivered studies on 12+ markets so far: the US, France, the EU, the UK, Eastern Europe, the UAE, Japan, China, Singapore, Vietnam, Thailand and Australia. Each study is built for one target market.
Which industries have you covered?
Studies delivered since 2022 covered travel tech, deep tech, advanced materials, cleantech, agritech and food, medtech, beauty, fintech, IoT, AI and SaaS, B2B platforms, and web3. The report structure stays the same, and I adapt the research to each product and target market.
What language are the deliverables in?
Reports are in English, as a slide deck. The kick-off, review and business matching meetings are also in English. I have coached 240+ Korean startups in English since 2022.
How does invoicing work? Can you issue a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서)?
Contracts and invoices go through Carte Blanche SAS, my French company. I invoice your organization directly, with an international commercial invoice instead of a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서). Korean program operators already accept it. For government-funded programs, I also provide the administrative documents requested during the contract and project period, including documents in Korean.
Can I get a written quote?
Yes. After the call, I send a quote based on the number of companies and target markets, with the documents your contract needs.
