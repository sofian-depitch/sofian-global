The deliverable

What's inside each report.

A comprehensive study built for each company's product and target market, delivered in presentation format. Made for market entry decisions and concrete first steps.

Market overview and trends

Market size, growth, and the trends that matter for the company's product in the target market.

Target market segments

Segment sizing and prioritization: where the product actually fits and who buys first.

Market access and regulation

Entry routes, approvals, duties, and the regulatory path, mapped before money is spent.

Competitive landscape

Local and global competitors, their pricing and positioning, and the gap the company can target.

Potential partners and distributors

Pre-identified and pre-contacted local partners, with named contacts and their level of interest.

Process

From kick-off to partner meetings.

A fixed scope over roughly 12 weeks per cohort, aligned with your program timeline and reporting deadlines.

01 01

Kick-off meeting

Week 1

A structured interview with each startup: product and assets, international strategy, target market, competition, barriers to entry, and regulation. It defines exactly what the research must answer.

02 02

Research and report

Weeks 2 to 8

In-depth research into the target market, delivered as a comprehensive report in presentation format.

03 03

Review meeting

Week 9

Walkthrough of the findings with the startup and the program team, and agreement on the market entry next steps.

04 04

Business matching

Weeks 10 to 12

2 to 3 meetings arranged and conducted with potential partners from the report, together with the startup.

FAQ

Common questions.

Which target markets can you cover?
12+ markets delivered so far: the US, France, the EU, the UK, Eastern Europe, the Middle East, Japan, China, Singapore, Vietnam, Thailand, and Australia. Each study is built for the specific target market.
Do you specialize in specific industries?
No, the format is sector agnostic. Studies delivered since 2022 covered travel tech, deep tech, advanced materials, cleantech, agritech and food, medtech, beauty, fintech, IoT, AI and SaaS, B2B platforms, and web3. The report structure stays the same. The research is adapted to each product and target market.
What language are the deliverables in?
Reports are delivered in English, in presentation format. Kick-off, review, and business matching meetings are also held in English, proven with Korean founders across 200+ sessions since 2019.
How does invoicing work? Can you issue a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서)?
Carte Blanche SAS is a French company, so I issue an international commercial invoice rather than a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서). The invoice can be issued directly to your organization. For government-funded programs, I also provide the additional administrative documents requested during the contract and project period, including documents prepared in Korean.
