FAQ

Common questions.

How many startups can you take in one cohort?
Up to 20 startups per coach. Larger cohorts are split into tracks, each with its own coach, as in the 2026 Innopolis program with a Bio track and a Deep Tech track.
What does the program team receive?
For each startup, the one-page POC plan and a report after each session, in your program's template, with the screenshots you need for your own reporting. In Global POC Program Management, you also receive the partner list with their problem statements, the screening results, meeting recaps and progress reports.
Can you bring the corporate partners?
Yes, in Global POC Program Management. It starts with the corporates' innovation teams and their problem statements, before the open call to startups. The network has 50+ corporate partners.
Which markets do you cover?
So far, programs have run in Singapore and Japan, with corporates in banking, transport and consumer goods. A program can also target Southeast Asia, Europe or the US.
Online or on site? In which language?
Coaching sessions run online, one hour each, and we manage the booking system for the whole cohort. Partner meetings and demo days can be online or on site. Sessions are in English, the language of the partner meeting, and administrative documents can be prepared in Korean.
How does invoicing work? Can you issue a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서)?
Contracts and invoices go through Carte Blanche SAS, my French company. I invoice your organization directly, with an international commercial invoice instead of a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서). Korean program operators already accept it. For government-funded programs, I also provide the administrative documents requested during the contract and project period, including documents in Korean.
Can I get a written quote?
Yes. After the call, I send a quote based on the format and the number of startups, with the documents your contract needs.
