The problem

Why your competitor is named

Its US distributor's page states the IP67 rating and the factory use case in English. ChatGPT cites that page.

Problem found

Your IP67 certification and test results sit in a PDF datasheet. Your English pages name the product, not the use case buyers ask about.

Fix proposed

An English answer page for the use case, with the certification and test data as text. Then a listing on the 2 directories ChatGPT cites.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the report really free?
Yes. One report is free, with no account and no payment details.
How is this different from SEO?
SEO works on where your pages rank in a list of links. GEO works on what AI answers: whether it names you, how it describes you, and which sources it cites. A page can rank well and never be cited. The report shows what AI cites instead, from real answers.
Why can't we just ask ChatGPT ourselves?
You can run the searches yourself, and you should. The work is deciding what to change, turning your evidence into publishable content, and tracking what happens after it goes live. I manage that process with your team. And one answer, asked from Seoul and logged in, says little about what buyers abroad see.
Is GEO useful if we have no local distributor yet?
Yes, if you already have a product, proof and a target market: the report shows which distributors, directories and media AI trusts there, which helps you choose. If you are still testing product-market fit, start with market entry basics first.
Which AI assistants do you measure?
All five from the baseline: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude. Then ChatGPT every week, under fixed conditions: from your market, logged out, 3 runs per question, every answer kept with the sources it cites. The four others every month. Claude is asked from a fresh account, since it cannot be used logged out. The free report covers ChatGPT. China is not included, because buyers there mostly use local AI assistants.
Do you guarantee results?
No. No one controls what an AI answers. I commit to the measurement, the actions and the written content. The first signs are answers that reuse your new wording or cite your new page, read over several weeks.
How much does it cost?
The GEO 3-month package starts at ₩4,990,000 per market, for the full 3 months. The final price depends on the number of markets and the scope. Paid ads, fake reviews and mass-produced content are never part of it.
How do contracts and invoicing work?
Contracts and invoices go through Carte Blanche SAS, my French company. I invoice your company directly, with an international commercial invoice instead of a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서). Korean program operators already accept it. When a public support program funds the work, I also provide the administrative documents it requires, including documents in Korean.
